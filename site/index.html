All Media Downloader

Paste a social media profile link and every photo on it is saved to a folder on your computer. It runs on your own machine, uses your own browser login when a site needs one, and uploads nothing.

Download the app (.zip) Build {{COMMIT}} from {{BRANCH}}, {{BUILT}}. Needs Python 3.10 or newer.

Set it up

  1. Unzip the download anywhere.
  2. Windows: double-click start.bat. macOS or Linux: run ./start.sh in a terminal.
  3. The first run installs what it needs into a private .venv folder, then opens http://127.0.0.1:8765.
  4. Paste a profile link. For Instagram and X, log in to the site in Firefox or Chrome first and pick that browser under "Use my login from".

Works with

InstagramNeeds your browser login. Strict rate limits, so try "Stop after" on big profiles.
X / TwitterNeeds your browser login.
Reddit, Tumblr, PinterestPublic profiles work without a login.
Everything elseThe sites gallery-dl supports, which does the downloading.

Why it isn't a website you just visit

Social sites block downloads from cloud servers and hide most content from logged-out visitors. Running on your computer means requests come from you, with your login, at a polite pace (one every 1 to 2.5 seconds). Only accounts you can already see are reachable.
